The Landmark Level of Service

From intake to the end of therapy, one team

  1. 1

    Intake

    Orders are received, insurance verified and the order qualified.

  2. 2

    Plan of care

    We work with the physician and discharge team on an individual IV treatment plan.

  3. 3

    Delivery & nursing

    Medications, equipment and supplies arrive home, with home health arranged.

  4. 4

    Ongoing support

    Labs, monitoring and clinical support 24/7 until therapy is complete.

About Landmark

Experience a Landmark Level of Service

Landmark Healthcare, Inc. was formed in 1999 with the mission of discharging patients to their homes on a timely basis and providing ongoing home support for their physician's plan of care. We fully coordinate orders with clinical referrals, home health, patients and caregivers, and provide full training and support for our products 24/7/365.

Who we serve

  • Patients receiving infusion, and their caregivers
  • Prescribing physicians and their clinical staff
  • Integrated care providers managing our patients' care
  • Health insurance payers seeking quality homecare
